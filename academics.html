Programmes offered

Choose your discipline

All programmes run over three years under the Ghana Education Service curriculum, combining core subjects with your elected field.

01

General Science

The pre-med and pre-engineering track. Students rotate through physics, chemistry and biology labs weekly, with elective mathematics as the engine of the programme.

Elective MathsPhysicsChemistryBiology
02

Technical

Our signature programme since 1982. Applied electricity, metalwork and technical drawing taught by experienced instructors in dedicated workshops on campus.

Applied ElectricityMetalworkTechnical DrawingElective Maths
03

Business

Accounting, costing and business management for future accountants, bankers and entrepreneurs of the Forces family.

Financial AccountingCost AccountingBusiness ManagementEconomics
04

General Arts

Government, literature and economics — the programme for future lawyers, journalists, diplomats and civic leaders.

GovernmentLiterature-in-EnglishEconomicsElective subjects vary
05

Home Economics

Food and nutrition, management in living and textiles — a science of the home and a gateway to hospitality, fashion and dietetics careers.

Food & NutritionManagement in LivingTextiles / Clothing
06

Visual Arts

Graphic design, sculpture and picture making in a studio culture that has produced designers, artists and creatives across Ghana.

Graphic DesignSculpturePicture Making
