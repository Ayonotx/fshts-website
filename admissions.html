Your place at Burma Camp starts here.

Admission into Forces SHTS is by the Computerised School Selection & Placement System (CSSPS) after the BECE — and it begins with one simple choice.

How to join

Four steps to Forces

Whether you are a BECE candidate or a parent, the path is the same — simple, free and transparent.

1 · Write the BECE

Complete the Basic Education Certificate Examination. Our students come from armed-forces schools, public schools and private schools across Ghana.

2 · Select Forces SHTS

During school selection, choose Forces Senior High Technical School, Burma Camp (Code 0010115 — Category B, Mixed, Day/Boarding) and your preferred programme.

3 · Receive your placement & admission letter

Placed students receive an admission letter with the prospectus, fees and reporting date. Watch the school's Facebook page and this site for updates.

4 · Report to campus

Come to Burma Camp, Accra with your items on the announced re-opening day. Orientation week introduces you to the Forces way of life.

What to bring

Reporting requirements

Full details are in the prospectus issued with each admission letter. Typically you will need:

  • ✔ Your admission letter and BECE results slip
  • ✔ Prospective student's personal items per the prospectus
  • ✔ Approved fees and PTA dues receipt
  • ✔ Boards & lodging supplies for boarders (chop box, bedding)
  • ✔ National ID / birth certificate copy

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Forces SHTS a boarding school?

We are a mixed, day and boarding school within Burma Camp. Boarders live in the barracks community's supervised accommodation; day students come from surrounding neighbourhoods.

What category is the school and what aggregate do I need?

Forces SHTS is a Category B school. CSSPS places students automatically by BECE aggregate and school choice — there is no separate entrance exam.

Which programme should I choose?

Choose the programme that matches your strengths and ambitions: Science for medicine/engineering, Technical for hands-on engineering, Business for finance, Arts for humanities, Home Economics or Visual Arts for creative careers. See the Academics page.

Do you accept students from outside the forces community?

Yes. While the school was founded for dependants of soldiers and Ministry of Defence staff, it serves the wider community — most of our students today come from civilian families.

How do I check results and school fees?

Parents and students use the school management portal — results, PTA bills and notices for all your children appear under one account. Open the portal ↗
