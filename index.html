Welcome to Forces SHTS

A community where students and teachers become one family

Established in 1978 and taking its Secondary Technical identity in 1982 — the year carried on our crest — Forces Senior High Technical School provides quality education to the dependants of soldiers, Ministry of Defence staff and the surrounding community.

From our current campus commissioned in 2016, we equip every student with the skills, knowledge, moral aptitude and discipline to excel in their chosen path.

Why Forces?

The Forces difference

Discipline is our tradition; innovation is our direction. Here's what sets a Forces student apart.

Military-grade discipline

Structure, punctuality and respect are a way of life at Burma Camp — habits that carry into careers and leadership.

Science & ICT focus

Laboratories and computer labs power our vision to be Ghana's model school for Science, ICT and Technical education.

Technical workshops

Real tools, real projects. Our technical students graduate with skills their hands remember.

Day & boarding

A safe residential community within the barracks perimeter, plus flexible day options for families nearby.

Leadership & service

From cadet corps to school prefects, students learn to serve first — the forces way.

Family & community

Dependants of soldiers, MoD civilians and neighbours study side by side as one Forces family.
